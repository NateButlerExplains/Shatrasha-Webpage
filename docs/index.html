Share who will be in the room, your event goals, and any topics you’d like to explore. We’ll discuss the focus and format before confirming an engagement.

What should I include in a speaking inquiry?+

Your event date or timeframe, location, audience, and preferred topic are a helpful start. If those details are still taking shape, tell us what you know so far.

How do we discuss availability and fees?+

Use the inquiry form below to start a conversation about your event. Availability, scope, travel, and fees are discussed directly before anything is confirmed.

Is a speaking session individual legal advice?+

Sessions are for general legal education. They do not provide advice on an individual matter or create an attorney-client relationship.
